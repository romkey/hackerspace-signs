Green bin

Plants only.

Yes: in here
  • Leaves, grass, weeds, vines
  • Flowers, plants, clippings
  • Branches under 4 in thick
  • Cut to 36 in long or shorter
  • Pumpkins, fallen fruit
No: keep out
  • Food scraps (compost bin)
  • Plastic bags of any kind
  • Dirt, rocks, sod, stumps
  • Lumber, pallets, sawdust
  • Pet waste, ashes, straw

Business compost takes food only, so plants get their own bin. Tip it in loose. Tie long branches with twine, not wire. Wet leaves get heavy fast, so keep it liftable.

Not collected with the others. When it’s full, take it to a yard debris recycler. Ask on Slack who has the truck.
