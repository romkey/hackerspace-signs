GarbageNorth Portland · business service

Gray bin

Sort it first.

Yes: in here
  • Plastic bags, wrap and film
  • Foam, peanuts, air pillows
  • Loose lids and bottle caps
  • Paper towels, napkins, tissues
  • Take-out boxes, cups, cutlery
No: keep out
  • Batteries of any kind
  • Electronics, cords, chargers
  • CFL tubes and bulbs
  • Paint, solvents, chemicals
  • Big piles of food scraps

City rule: no recyclables in the garbage. Bag first: broken glass, ceramics, shredded paper, ashes, sawdust, packaging peanuts, pet waste. Seal oil and grease. Everything on the right needs a drop-off or another bin.

Bins go out Wednesday night. Back inside by Thursday morning. They can’t be left in the street.

Mixed recyclingNorth Portland · business service

Blue bin

Empty, dry, loose.

Yes: in here
  • Flattened cardboard, paper
  • Metal cans, balled-up foil
  • Plastic bottles, jugs and tubs
  • Cartons, clean pizza boxes
  • Scrap metal under 18 in
No: keep out
  • Glass (use the yellow bin)
  • Plastic bags, wrap, rings
  • Shredded paper
  • Cups, clamshells, foam
  • Anything under 2 in square

Tip it in loose. One bagged load gets the whole cart pulled as garbage. Caps on only if they screw on. Ball foil up baseball-size. Scrap metal under 10 lb.

Bins go out Wednesday night. Back inside by Thursday morning. They can’t be left in the street.

CompostNorth Portland · business service

Green bin

Food scraps only.

Yes: in here
  • Any food, plate scrapings
  • Meat, bones, dairy, bread
  • Grounds, peels, eggshells
  • Coffee filters, tea bags
  • Liners: BPI-certified only
No: keep out
  • Yard debris, leaves, prunings
  • Napkins, food-soiled paper
  • Anything “compostable”
  • Cups, bowls, cutlery, trays
  • Grease, oil, standing liquid

Business compost is food only, not the same as your green bin at home. “Biodegradable” and “made from plants” are garbage here, and so are napkins.

Bins go out Wednesday night. Back inside by Thursday morning. They can’t be left in the street.

GlassNorth Portland · business service

Yellow bin

Bottles and jars only.

Yes: in here
  • Glass bottles, any colour
  • Glass jars, any colour
  • Labels can stay on
  • Rinsed out, lids off
No: keep out
  • Broken glass of any kind
  • Lids, caps and corks
  • Drinking glasses and Pyrex
  • Dishware, ceramics, vases
  • Window glass, mirrors, bulbs

Broken glass cuts the crew who empty this bin. Bag it and put it in the gray bin. Same for everything else on the right. The city requires this bin to be collected separately from the blue one.

Bins go out Wednesday night. Back inside by Thursday morning. They can’t be left in the street.
