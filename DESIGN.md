# CTRL-H Sign Design Language

The rules for every sign, label and poster at **PDX Hackerspace (CTRL-H)**.
Read this before making a sign. If a rule gets in the way of a sign doing its job,
the job wins. Then come back and fix the rule.

> **The short version:** dark header band with the mark → rainbow trail → one big,
> plain message on warm paper → one small critter in its own corner → a quiet footer.
> **Readable first, whimsical second, and never the other way round.**

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Say it in one glance.** The person reading is holding a door, a spool of filament or a bag of trash. The main message must be readable in about 2 seconds from 6 ft away.
2. **Instruction first, joke second.** The whimsy goes *next to* the information, never *in* it. If the joke were removed, the sign would still be complete.
3. **One critter per sign.** One sasquatch, alien, LED or hexagon flourish. Two is a sticker sheet.
4. **Colour means something.** The whole rainbow together is decoration (it's *us*). A single rainbow colour on its own always carries meaning (§3).
5. **Built from hexagons.** Bullets, accents, frames and badges are hexagons. Corners are square or hexagonal, never rounded.
6. **Made here.** Signs should look like they could be laser-cut, engraved, stitched or backlit, because they will be (§9).

---

## 2. The mark

The mark is a hexagon holding a **caret built from five small hexagons** and a **capital H with pointed (hexagonal) ends**: `^H`, the backspace control code. Hence CTRL-H.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `brand/logo/ctrlh-mark.svg` | Caret + H, single colour (`currentColor`). Footers, small labels, stamps. |
| `brand/logo/ctrlh-mark-rainbow.svg` | Caret + H in brand colours. Sign headers on ink. |
| `brand/logo/ctrlh-badge.svg` | Full badge on ink: frame slots, PDX HACKERSPACE, EST. 2014. Posters, stickers, hero use. |
| `brand/logo/ctrlh-badge-mono.svg` | Full badge as one colour with mounting holes. Laser, CNC, vinyl, embroidery digitising. |
| `brand/logo/ctrlh-hex-outline.svg` | Just the outer hexagon. Die-cut and frame lines. |
| `brand/logo/logo-outline-white.webp` | Original white line-art (transparent). |
| `brand/reference/*` | The badge as built: LED backlit, brushed metal, embroidered patch, engraved plywood. |

Rules:
- **Don't redraw the mark.** All logo SVGs are generated by `tools/build_brand.py` (geometry traced from the plywood sign). Change the script, not the SVGs.
- Caret hexagon colours in full-colour use: lone hex **blue**, top row **yellow, red**, column **orange, violet**. H runs **red → violet top to bottom**.
- Hexagons are **pointy-top**, always.
- Minimum size: mark 0.3 in tall printed; full badge 2 in wide (the text gets mushy below that).
- Clear space around the mark: the width of one caret hexagon.
- The badge text is set in Space Grotesk 600. If you need it as outlines for fabrication, convert text to paths in Inkscape first.

## 3. Colour

Sampled from the Nyan mural and tuned for print. Tokens live in `brand/tokens.css`.

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--hs-ink` | `#16181D` | Text, outlines, header bands. Our "black". |
| `--hs-paper` | `#FBF8F1` | Default sign background. Warm white, not pure white. |
| `--hs-night` | `#1F5A85` | Mural sky blue. Alternate dark background for special signs. |
| `--hs-steel` | `#A9B4B6` | Brushed metal. Rules, LED legs, secondary text on ink. |
| `--hs-muted` | `#5B616B` | Secondary text on paper. |
| `--hs-red` | `#E2382D` | **No / forbidden / danger** |
| `--hs-orange` | `#F28A0F` | **Ask first / warning / conditional** |
| `--hs-yellow` | `#F7CB2D` | **Heads up / caution** |
| `--hs-green` | `#6DB33F` | **Yes / allowed / done / goes here** |
| `--hs-blue` | `#119FD6` | **Info / notice** |
| `--hs-violet` | `#7468C2` | **Community / people** |

Rules:
- **All six together = brand.** Use the rainbow trail, rainbow gradient on the mark, or the six-colour hex cluster. Decoration only.
- **One colour alone = meaning.** Green means OK. Red means no. Don't use red for decoration on a sign that also has a red "don't" list.
- Text is ink on paper or paper on ink. **Never put body text on a rainbow colour**, except ink on yellow/orange and white on red/blue in safety headers.
- Never use colour as the only signal. Pair it with a word or icon (✓ / ✗, YES / NO, LED + label). Some members are colour-blind.

## 4. Sign families

Every sign belongs to one family. The family sets the **accent colour** (header hex, rule, bullets) and how much whimsy is allowed. Set it with `data-family` on `.sign`.

| Family | Accent | Examples | Whimsy |
|---|---|---|---|
| `safety` | red | Fire extinguisher, eye protection, machine hazards | **Minimal.** Optional: the goggled sasquatch, *only* modelling the correct behaviour, never in the hazard panel. No jokes in the text. |
| `tools` | orange | 3D printer filaments, laser materials, tool use | Normal |
| `house` | yellow | Pull the door closed, lights off, clean up | Full |
| `green` | green | Trash, recycling, compost | Normal |
| `wayfinding` | blue | Table names/numbers, room labels, "this way" | Normal, often the resistor or LED |
| `community` | violet | Volunteer list, events, open-house info | Full |

## 5. Typography

| Role | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display / titles | **Space Grotesk** 700 | Geometric and techy, close to the badge lettering |
| Body | **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** 400/700 | Designed for low vision. Body text always uses this. |
| Data / settings | **JetBrains Mono** 500 | Temperatures, part numbers, wattages, `#slack-channels` |
| Whimsy labels | **Silkscreen** | Pixel font. Speech bubbles and tiny jokes only, never instructions. |

All are free (OFL) from Google Fonts. `brand/tokens.css` loads them.

Sizes on a letter-size wall sign (read from 3–6 ft):

| Element | Size |
|---|---|
| One-word command (`.big-word`: PUSH, PULL, STOP) | 160–230 pt |
| Title (`.title`) | 60–80 pt |
| Subtitle | 40–48 pt |
| Body / list items | **24 pt minimum**, 28 pt preferred |
| Footer | 11 pt |

Rules of thumb: 1 in of capital-letter height per 10 ft of viewing distance. Sentence case for everything except the one-word command and header kickers. Left-aligned, never justified. Never centre more than three lines.

## 6. Layout

Every sign uses the same skeleton (`templates/sign.css`):

```
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│ ▓ HEADER: mark · KICKER      ⬢   │  ink band, accent hexagon on the right
├──────────────────────────────────┤
│ ▀▄▀▄▀▄ rainbow trail ▀▄▀▄▀▄▀▄▀▄▀ │  stepped, like the mural
├──────────────────────────────────┤
│                                  │
│  BIG MESSAGE                     │  big word or title
│  short subtitle.                 │
│  ▬▬▬ accent rule                 │
│  ⬢ point one                     │  hexagon or LED bullets
│  ⬢ point two                     │
│                                  │
│  ┌ confirm / key action ┐  👾    │  .row-end: last block + critter column
├──────────────────────────────────┤
│ ^H PDX HACKERSPACE · CTRL-H   id │  footer: sign id on the right
└──────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Page:** US Letter, portrait unless the content wants landscape. 0.5 in printer-safe margin.
- **Spacing unit:** ⅛ in (`--hs-u`). Use multiples.
- **The critter gets its own column** (`.row-end`) or its own empty corner. It never overlaps text and never sits behind it.
- **Footer** carries the mark, the name, and a sign id (`family / name`) so anyone can find
  the source file in this repo. Where the family name also means something else *on that
  sign*, use `topic / name` instead. The bin labels read `bins / glass-yellow`, not
  `green / bins-glass`, because "green" there would be the family, a bin and an LED at once.
  The family is still set with `data-family`; it just isn't the thing printed.
- Small labels (table tents, machine tags) keep the header colour band and the trail, and drop the footer.

**Landscape sort cards** (`signs/bins`, `.sign.landscape`) are the bin-label variant: title and
accent rule across the top, then two side-by-side lists (`.sort-col.yes` with a green LED and
the word YES, `.sort-col.no` with a red LED and the word NO), a muted `.sort-note` clarifier
under both, and a `.note` box plus the critter on the last row. Landscape loses 2.5 in of
height, so `.sign.landscape` tightens the header, trail and footer; keep bullets to one line
each and at most five per column or the footer falls off the page.

**Right / wrong pairs** (`.compare`, `signs/printers/bunker-storage.html`) show one drawing twice, the way it
should look and the way it should not. Each panel heads its picture with an LED and the word
RIGHT or WRONG, so the colour never signals alone, and carries a one-line caption under it.
The written list still does the teaching: the pair is there for the two-second read. A sign
built around one adds `class="sign diagram"`, which pulls the title down to 40 pt and tightens
the list and the confirm box to leave the pictures room on letter portrait. Three panels across
(two RIGHT states and one WRONG) go on `class="sign landscape diagram"`, which shortens the
pictures, runs the list in two columns and slims the confirm box so it all fits 11 x 8.5. A confirm box that must not be skipped (training
required) takes `.check.alert`: red fill, paper text, red LED.

On a label stuck to a physical thing that already has a colour (a bin), the header's
`.accent-hex` is replaced by a `.bin-badge`: a hexagon in *that object's* colour next to its
name in words (GRAY BIN, BLUE BIN). The colour is set per sign as `--bin: var(--hs-…)`, never
a raw hex, and never travels without the words. The family accent still owns the rule and the
bullets.

## 7. Whimsy kit

All in `brand/illustrations/` and `brand/patterns/`, generated by `tools/build_brand.py`.

**Characters**, drawn as chunky pixel sprites with ink outlines like the Nyan mural. Render with `image-rendering: pixelated`.

| | File | Personality / where it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Sasquatch | `sasquatch.svg` | Local legend, big, helpful, a bit clumsy. House rules, doors, heavy things, cleaning up. |
| Sasquatch in goggles | `sasquatch-safety-goggles.svg` | The *only* critter allowed on safety signs, showing the correct behaviour. |
| Alien | `alien.svg` | Curious newcomer who doesn't know the rules yet. Onboarding, "how to", the outside of doors. |
| Saucer | `saucer.svg` | Arrivals, wayfinding, "you are here". |
| Space cat | `spacecat.svg` | Our own mascot: an orange-and-white cat astronaut in a glass helmet with antenna and collar LEDs. Events, community, "welcome", anything space-themed. Pairs well with sparkles on `--hs-night`. |
| Sparkle | `sparkle.svg`, `sparkle-white.svg` | The mural stars. Scatter 2–5 on dark backgrounds. |
| Pixel hex | `hex-pixel.svg` | A tiny pixel accent. |
| Table symbols | `kitty`, `ninja`, `doggie`, `pirate`, `robot`, `llama` (+ `alien`) | Each table's mascot (`signs/tables`). Used only for that table; they're its name. |

**Electronics**, as clean vector shapes:

| | File | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| LED | `led-green/amber/red/blue/yellow/violet.svg` | **Status lights.** Green = yes / done / allowed. Amber = ask first. Red = no. Use them as bullets in yes/no lists. |
| Resistor | `resistor.svg`, `resistor-rainbow.svg` | Dividers, and **table numbers in colour code** (Table 4 = a yellow band; 0 black, 1 brown, 2 red, 3 orange, 4 yellow, 5 green, 6 blue, 7 violet, 8 grey, 9 white). Always print the number too. |

**Equipment diagrams**, vector like the electronics, drawn front-on in ink, paper and steel:

| | File | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Filament bunker | `bunker-latched.svg`, `bunker-open.svg` | The cereal-box dry box in storage configuration: catch clipped and the PTFE tube looped from one lid fitting to the other, or lid up with the catch open and one tube end out. Same geometry in both, so a `.compare` pair differs only where the sign is making its point. |

A diagram is information, not whimsy, so it doesn't use up the sign's one critter.

**Table ID signs** (`signs/tables/build_tables.py`) are the one place a critter is the content, not decoration: coordinate, symbol, name, and the coordinate in resistor colours. Add tables by editing `TABLES` in that script.

**Patterns:** `rainbow-trail.svg` (the stepped Nyan trail, used as the header strip) and `honeycomb.svg` (tiling hex outline for backgrounds at ≤10% opacity).

**Speech bubbles** (`.bubble`): Silkscreen, 1–2 words, all caps: `CLICK!`, `BEEP`, `NOM`, `THX!`.

### About the Nyan mural
PDX Hackerspace has permission to use Nyan Cat, so the cat is part of the kit, not just the trail.
- Use `brand/mural/nyan-mural.jpg` freely for posters, the website and social media.
- On signs, Nyan Cat is a critter like any other: one per sign, in its own corner or column, never on safety signs. It fits best on `house` and `community` signs, and anywhere near the mural.
- Use the cat from an existing source: a crop of the mural photo, or official Nyan Cat art saved as `brand/illustrations/nyan-cat.*` (record where it came from in `brand/illustrations/SOURCES.md`). Don't redraw, restyle or recolour it.
- The rainbow trail and sparkles still work on their own when the cat would be too much.

## 8. Voice and copy

- **Title: 1–5 words, verb first.** "Pull until it clicks." "Bag it first." "Ask before cutting."
- **Max 5 bullets.** If you need more, it's a manual. Put a link or QR code to the wiki.
- **Say why in one short line** when the why changes behaviour ("The closer won't latch it for you.").
- **Concrete numbers in mono:** `PLA 200–220 °C`, `max 6 mm`, `40 W`.
- **Dos before don'ts.** Lead with what *is* allowed.
- **Jokes are dry and short**, and they live in the bubble, kicker or footer. Never make a joke out of a safety instruction.
- Address the reader as "you". No "please do not…" walls of text. No ALL CAPS sentences.
- Name a person or channel for questions: "Questions? `#laser` on Slack".

### 8.1 No AI tells

Every sign has to read like a member wrote it on the way past the machine. Generated text
has a smell, and once a reader catches it they stop believing the sign. Every rule below
applies to sign copy. The dash, emoji and word rules apply to this repo's own prose too
(`DESIGN.md`, `README.md`, build-script docstrings), because that is where the next sign
gets its wording.

<!-- house-style: ignore start -->

**Punctuation**

- **No em dashes.** Not the character (—), not `&mdash;`, not ` -- `. Use a full stop, a comma,
  a colon, or two sentences. "Food scraps only, not yard debris." not "Food scraps only &mdash; not yard debris."
- **No en dash as a dash either.** The en dash is only for a span of numbers: `200–220 °C`,
  `3–6 ft`, `1–5 words`.
- **No semicolons.** A sign is not a legal brief. Split the sentence.
- **No colon-dash labels** like `Note:`, `Remember:`, `Pro tip:`, `Important:`. The `.note` box,
  the amber LED and the accent rule already do that job. A label that is itself the instruction
  or a real attribution (`Bag first:`, `City rule:`) is fine.
- **No trailing ellipsis** at the end of a sentence, and no `(s)` plurals.
- Arrows (→) are for sequences in a diagram, never inside a sentence.
- **No emoji, anywhere.** We have pixel critters, LEDs and hexagons. Dingbats
  (➜ ✦ ✨ 🚀 ✅) count as emoji.

<!-- house-style: ignore end -->

**Sentence shapes to avoid**

- "It's not just X, it's Y." Also "not only … but also", and "X isn't about Y, it's about Z."
- Negated-parallel emphasis: "This isn't a suggestion, it's a rule." Just state the rule.
- Rule-of-three for rhythm: "fast, simple and reliable". Three words are fine when all three
  carry information ("Empty, dry, loose"), wrong when the third is padding.
- "From X to Y" framing, and "Think of it as …".
- Rhetorical questions as openers: "Ever wondered why the door won't latch?"
- Closing flourishes: "Happy hacking!", "Thanks for keeping the space awesome", "We appreciate
  your cooperation", "Thank you for your understanding".

<!-- house-style: ignore start -->

**Words we don't print**

simply, just (as a softener), seamless, effortless, robust, leverage, utilize, ensure, delve,
foster, elevate, unlock, streamline, crucial, vital, essential, best practices, journey,
game-changer, at the end of the day, kindly.

Say the thing instead: "Make sure it's latched" beats "ensure proper latching".

<!-- house-style: ignore end -->

**Emphasis and hedging**

- **One bold phrase per block at most.** Bolding three things in a row bolds nothing.
- No hedging on an instruction. "Generally, batteries shouldn't go in here" is not a rule.
  "No batteries" is.
- Sentence case for headings and bullets. Title Case On A Headline reads as generated.

`tools/lint_signs.py` checks the mechanical rules above (dashes, semicolons, emoji, `(s)`
plurals, colon labels, the word list) on every sign in CI, and `tools/lint_docs.py` checks
the dash, emoji and word rules on this repo's prose. The rest is a human job. The two lists
of rules in this section are wrapped in a `<!-- house-style: ignore start -->` marker,
because a rule has to be able to name what it bans.

**The read-aloud test.** Say the sign out loud. If it sounds like a helpful assistant rather
than the person who fixed the door closer last week, cut it and write it again shorter.

## 9. Making signs in different materials

The same design has to survive the laser, the CNC, the embroidery machine and the printer.

| Medium | Rules |
|---|---|
| **Paper / laminated** | Default. Print from the PDF at 100%, no "fit to page". Laminate anything near water or the kitchen. |
| **Laser engrave** (ply, acrylic) | Single colour: use `*-mono.svg` / `ctrlh-mark.svg`. No gradients, no sprites smaller than 1 px = 1 mm. Text ≥ 6 mm cap height. Lines ≥ 0.5 mm. |
| **Laser / CNC cut-through** (backlit) | Letters need stencil bridges on closed counters (A, D, O, P, R). Slot gaps ≥ 3 mm. Keep the six frame slots: they're the light bars. |
| **Vinyl** | Single colour per layer. Min stroke 1 mm. Avoid pixel sprites below 3 mm per pixel. |
| **Embroidery** | Badge only, ≥ 3 in. Satin-stitch outline in ink. Small text (EST. 2014) ≥ 6 mm tall. |

## 10. Checklist before you print

- [ ] The main message reads from 6 ft in under 2 seconds.
- [ ] Belongs to a family; accent colour matches.
- [ ] One critter at most, not covering anything.
- [ ] Single colours carry meaning *and* are paired with words or icons.
- [ ] Body text ≥ 24 pt, Atkinson Hyperlegible.
- [ ] No em dashes, no emoji, no AI tells (§8.1). Read it aloud.
- [ ] Footer has the sign id.
- [ ] Safety signs: no jokes in the text, ANSI-style header (`.safety-header.danger|warning|caution|notice`).
- [ ] Rendered with `tools/render.sh` and checked the PNG.
